Behind the dashboard

A short technical overview of the sensor, firmware, and server behind this page. (In construction)

Hardware

Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 with an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, battery-powered — no mains power or permanent WiFi needed.

Firmware

Deep-sleeps between wakes, takes a reading on a timer, posts it over HTTPS. Failed sends are queued on-device (up to 48h) and flushed later. No onboard clock — timestamps are reconstructed server-side.

Server

FastAPI + SQLite. The device only sends a raw distance reading — level/volume conversion, trend tracking, and projections all happen server-side.

Dashboard

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, charted with lightweight-charts. Read-only, no account or tracking — just a cookie for display preferences.
